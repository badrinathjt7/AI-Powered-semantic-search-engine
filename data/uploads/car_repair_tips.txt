Car Repair Tips - Example Corpus File

This document contains simple explanations for fixing common car issues:

1. Engine overheating:
   - Check coolant levels
   - Ensure the radiator fan is working
   - Inspect hoses for leaks

2. Car won't start:
   - Test battery voltage
   - Check starter motor connection
   - Inspect spark plugs

3. Brake noise:
   - Check brake pads
   - Clean brake rotors
   - Inspect calipers

4. Low mileage:
   - Check tire pressure
   - Replace air filters
   - Inspect fuel injectors

5. AC not cooling:
   - Recharge refrigerant
   - Inspect AC compressor
   - Check coolant sensor
